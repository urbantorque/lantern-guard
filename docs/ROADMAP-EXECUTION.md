## Phase 0

Current and historical chapter/endurance records now share a decoder. District restoration derives credit from saved records, repairing affected profiles without inventing wins or awarding duplicate currency. Practice records and unknown formats are excluded. Existing saves and earned cosmetics are retained.

Settings offers opt-in local playtest recording, JSON export and clear. Recording retains eight sessions with at most 600 events each. Game purchases, actions, wave results and leaks are captured without personal information or network transmission. Recording and storage failures cannot block gameplay.

## Validation boundaries

## Phase 1

First Lights introduces Mira, sparks, overlapping Chime control and a held pulse across six authored waves. Commands appear from wave 4. The first win reopens the Night Market shutters and records one third of its restoration. New players can start directly from the title; campaign selection and advanced watches remain available. Mission rules, source timings, loadout, wave data and records are versioned independently of existing watches.

The paid reference completed with 17 light and one released command in 149 simulation seconds, excluding player planning time. This is not evidence of the proposed six-to-eight-minute human session length. Exact save continuation and malformed mission rejection pass. Browser checks passed the first purchase, guided flow, campaign, reward, save/resume and local JSON export at 1280×800, 390×844, 320×740 and 844×390.

Score targets are design ambitions, not automatically earned by shipping features. Observed newcomer/returning-player sessions and physical iPhone performance, thermals and sound evaluation require people and hardware. Their protocol and unverified status will accompany the final release. Browser and simulation evidence will be reported separately.

## Scope constraints

Eight tower roles, three upgrade stages, three keepers, predictable combat routes and cosmetic permanent rewards remain the foundation. Campaign content uses the existing four waterways. No energy timers, additional currencies, equipment grind or multiplayer are part of this delivery.

## Phases 2–4

Eight missions now alternate armour, hidden fleets, split entrances, repair escorts, close formations and isolated heavies before combining them in Last Bloom. Revealing arches use the actual reveal timers. Passage choices in missions 7 and 8 are permanent between-wave constructions; save restoration preserves their inlet timing. Other missions keep a single authored route.

Placement previews sample the real paths and effective range, showing upper/final/side coverage, shared support water and the position’s tradeoff. Ivo can mark an enemy on the battlefield or use a target list. Held-command readouts explicitly identify the paused tower. Existing six signatures have authored favourable and unfavourable encounters throughout the campaign.

The paid campaign matrix covers three keepers, two compositions and both passages where applicable. It records 49/54 wins. Every keeper has a winning paid reference on every mission. Failures are retained: chain builds struggle against some finales; an overly dispersed Undertow opening fails Last Bloom’s side approach. These are diagnostic bot strategies, not human win-rate estimates. Every run validates planning and mid-wave snapshots, exact continuation, and source timing after passage restoration.

Each district project has three visible stages. Specific mission wins repair shutters, awnings, a lens and dome, terraces and planting before the final illumination. Nessa, Edda and Jori give the results personal context. Older watch achievements retain their restoration credit. Practice earns no permanent progress. The campaign result replaces the timed generic celebration and duplicate report with one restoration scene, a next-mission action and an optional detailed report.

Opening, campaign selection, first restoration, save/resume and local log export pass at 1280×800, 390×844, 320×740 and 844×390. Physical-device and observed-player validation remains separate.

## Phase 5

Crown hardware is now specific to each weapon: larger focusing mirrors, Frost Chime crystals, satellite coils or a capacitor ring, reinforced launchers, expanded wings, elevated garden frames and bolt supports. Art follows the existing three-stage gameplay rather than adding upgrade tiers. Portrait framing is checked for both specialisations across all three keepers.

Small hostile silhouettes receive a bounded phone-size readability adjustment. Heavy creatures rock with their gait; flyers and healers hover; slowing changes animation cadence and stun holds the current pose instead of snapping to a neutral frame. Hitboxes, travel and range remain unchanged. Each waterway has a distinct ground palette, recessed water and sharper stone edges. Fog is lighter over combat. Lobbed rounds cast moving ground shadows, bolts have a darker shaft and brighter edge, and deliberate commands receive a single distinct release accent.

Targeting help lives in the control bar so it cannot cover the enemy being selected. Portrait controls stack and landscape controls use two bounded columns. Target buttons retain 44-pixel hit areas. The first mission no longer advertises a side inlet opening at wave 99; mission-specific landmark copy describes the actual mission.

The render suite validates 207 unclipped portraits, eight animated tower roles, reduced-motion tower/projectile/beam states, projectile endpoints in both cameras, and no mutation of gameplay during rendering. Sixteen synthetic stress scenes cover all four maps, both cameras and day/night with 12 towers and 80 enemies. Browser release checks cover direct selection, list fallback, release, larger text, reduced motion, nine restoration stages and final combat at four viewport sizes. The UI targeting fixture uses a paid layout with synthetic durable enemies; paid balance evidence comes from the separate simulation matrix.

## Simplification decisions

The eight-mission campaign is the main progression path. Long watches and endurance are advanced options; the opening exposes two tower roles and delays the command until it has a purpose. Mission victories use one persistent restoration result instead of a timed generic celebration followed by a second full report. Detailed statistics remain available on demand.

No score target is presented as achieved. [The player/device protocol](PLAYER-VALIDATION.md) defines the observed tests needed to judge enjoyment, mastery, retention intent, sound fatigue and sustained native performance.
