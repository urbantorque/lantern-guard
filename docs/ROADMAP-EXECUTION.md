## Phase 0

Current and historical chapter/endurance records now share a decoder. District restoration derives credit from saved records, repairing affected profiles without inventing wins or awarding duplicate currency. Practice records and unknown formats are excluded. Existing saves and earned cosmetics are retained.

Settings offers opt-in local playtest recording, JSON export and clear. Recording retains eight sessions with at most 600 events each. Game purchases, actions, wave results and leaks are captured without personal information or network transmission. Recording and storage failures cannot block gameplay.

## Phase 1

First Lights introduces Mira, sparks, overlapping Chime control and a held pulse across six authored waves. Commands appear from wave 4. The first win reopens the Night Market shutters and records one third of its restoration. New players can start directly from the title; campaign selection and advanced watches remain available. Mission rules, source timings, loadout, wave data and records are versioned independently of existing watches.

The paid reference completed with 17 light and one released command in 149 simulation seconds, excluding player planning time. This is not evidence of the proposed six-to-eight-minute human session length. Exact save continuation and malformed mission rejection pass. Browser checks passed the first purchase, guided flow, campaign, reward, save/resume and local JSON export at 1280×800, 390×844, 320×740 and 844×390.

Score targets are design ambitions, not automatically earned by shipping features. Observed newcomer/returning-player sessions and physical iPhone performance, thermals and sound evaluation require people and hardware. Their protocol and unverified status will accompany the final release. Browser and simulation evidence will be reported separately.

## Scope constraints

Eight tower roles, three upgrade stages, three keepers, predictable combat routes and cosmetic permanent rewards remain the foundation. Campaign content uses the existing four waterways. No energy timers, additional currencies, equipment grind or multiplayer are part of this delivery.
