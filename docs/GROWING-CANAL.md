# Growing canal revision

## Player experience

The title now has a single Play or Continue button. Difficulty is a secondary control. Guide, Collection and Settings are the only navigation links; journals, feats and unlocked daily/weekly challenges live in Collection. There is no map picker.

New ordinary nights use a versioned growing canal. The Great Lantern stays at the same position. Tower identities, pads, investments, cooldowns, upgrades and bank blooms survive each expansion.

| Available before | Section | Building pads | Locks |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Wave 1 | Lantern bend, beside the Great Lantern | 6 | Lower Lock, usable from wave 2 |
| Wave 6 | Upper canal and Lantern bridge | 14 | Both locks |
| Wave 11 | West inlet and outer banks | 18 | Both locks, two entrances |

The first section is an actual shorter route. The camera fits that section; expanding upstream reveals the full canal. Future paths and pads cannot be tapped, selected with a keyboard or built on. Expansion occurs only with an empty canal. Early calls across waves 5/6 and 10/11 are blocked until all overlapping waves clear. Auto-start waits for a manual Start after each expansion, including after a reload.

| Tower | Always-visible role | Available before |
|---|---|---|
| Wickling | Fast shots | Wave 1 |
| Cracker | Crowd bursts | Wave 1 |
| Moonbell | Slows groups | Wave 3 |
| Glow Garden | Earns glow | Wave 4 |
| Lamp Owl | Sees hidden | Wave 6 |
| Lighthouse | Heavy beam | Wave 7 |

Selection explains the role in a sentence. Future towers stay out of the tray until available. Charms appear from wave 8 on modes that support them. The guide includes the expansion schedule and tower introductions. The Reed lantern cosmetic is earned by completing all 25 waves of a growing night; previous unlocks remain valid.

## Compatibility

New ordinary nights use `challenge.expanding: 1`. Their full snapshots contain `canalStage`, validated against the wave and active enemies. Legacy Wickwater/Reedbank saves and daily/weekly challenges keep their original geometry and availability. Existing saves are not silently restarted or converted. Start a new night to use the new progression.

Tower stats, prices, upgrades, enemy stats, wave enemy counts, spawn timings, ordinary reward budgets and difficulty multipliers are unchanged. Two lock introductions and map/tower availability are intentionally rescheduled for new growing nights.

## Verification

- Original simulation and release checks pass; 15 legacy Wickwater replays match the original hashes and outcomes.
- Growing-canal tests check simulation-level build restrictions, stable tower identities and upgrades, fixed Lantern position, both expansion boundaries, early-call barriers, manual planning breaks and source visibility.
- Mid-wave and between-wave saves round-trip exactly in every section. Simulations on each side of both save/resume boundaries remain identical. Invalid progression saves are rejected.
- Real-play snapshots generated by `npm run test:growth` support development-only browser checks at `/qa/`.
- Browser checks cover new-night menu flow, 320/375-pixel portrait with larger text, landscape, building, upgrades, both live reveals, reload and the auto-start planning hold. Rotation refits the next-wave preview. The final production menu has no console warnings or errors.
- Production build and iOS asset sync are checked separately. This Windows validation does not compile or run the native iOS app.

## Balance observations

The [36-run matrix](GROWING-CANAL-BALANCE.jsonl) covers six strategies, three difficulties and seeds 7/31. These bots are comparison tools, not human win-rate estimates.

| Strategy | Relaxed | Standard | Nightfall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Balanced with routing | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 |
| Balanced without routing | 2/2 | 0/2 | 0/2 |
| Heavy hitters with routing | 2/2 | 0/2 | 0/2 |
| Three early gardens | 2/2 | 0/2 | 0/2 |
| Slow reactions (1.5 seconds) | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 |
| Careful sorter, long-range beams | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 |

All Standard strategies cleared the first five waves without losing light. Standard still requires deliberate later coverage; the routing, slower-reaction and beam strategies won both seeds. An eight-run [focused Nightfall follow-up](GROWING-CANAL-NIGHTFALL.json) found one win with two gardens and concentrated beam upgrades. Nightfall remains demanding and needs human playtesting, especially the final boss and the effect of early tower placement on later coverage. No enemy or combat nerfs were introduced to make these checks pass.
