# Lantern Guard: continuous journeys

Implemented 27 September 2026. The first 25 waves keep their existing layout, tower prices, upgrade paths and Guard rules. Original saved games remain on their original rules; the 15 original replay comparisons still match exactly.

## What changed

### 1. Make the board explain itself

- A skippable first-build demonstration previews a Wickling on an actual pad and highlights the water in range. The player still buys the tower with the normal starting budget.
- The prompt advances through placement, starting the wave and watching coverage. It stays out of older and challenge saves.
- Armour, revealed hidden enemies and slowing have distinct combat cues. Tower panels show actual damage, kills or their existing support contribution.
- A leak records the enemy, branch, remaining armour/hidden state and light cost. The planning break and defeat screen use that observed fact for one concrete suggestion. Auto-start waits after a leak.

### 2. Preserve the investment in a defence

- Campaign and challenge runs have independent saves, previous-save recovery and journal credit. The title can continue either.
- Existing single-slot challenges migrate with their flowers and credit, including the older separate-save format. Failed migration writes preserve the source.
- Relaxed and Standard ordinary Guard games save a planning checkpoint immediately before each wave. Defeat offers **Revise from wave N**, restoring the exact towers, upgrades, routes, glow, light and flowers. Players can alter the build before trying again.
- Retries are unavailable in Nightfall, scored challenges and free play. Replayed journal counts use a high-water mark so they cannot award the same enemies twice. A retryable failed attempt does not award another completed-night result.
- A completed campaign remains saved. Reloading its result does not grant the completion reward again.

### 3. Lantern Harbour

At wave-25 dawn, **Open Lantern Harbour** adds eight authored waves and four upstream build pads. All existing towers, upgrades, routes, light and glow stay in place. A view switch moves between the Harbour and canal, with a warning count when enemies approach the lantern out of view.

Skiffs accelerate when their armour breaks. The Harbour Warden launches Skiffs and surges once at half health. The eight compositions alternate crowds, hidden escorts, armour and pressure from both entrances. Each wave waits for a clear board and a manual start.

The Harbour has its own completion counter. It does not award the canal's win or blooms again. Free play can start after either the original dawn or Harbour completion and counts from the chosen endpoint.

### 4. One optional guardian

**Ember Keeper** unlocks through the existing Crowned feat and is selected in Collection. New ordinary nights use that choice; saved nights keep their original guardian and challenges use Lantern Keeper.

Cracker impact damage is reduced by 40%. Main explosions leave fire for 2.4 seconds at 40% of the tower's damage per second. Overlapping patches use the strongest patch only, capped at 48 active patches. Mini explosions do not create more ground fire. Fire respects hidden-enemy visibility and persists through saves.

Costs, the six tower slots and upgrade paths are unchanged. The controlled combat regression verifies lower initial impact but greater total damage when a target stays in the fire. Fast-moving enemies and immediate threats still favour the default Cracker.

### 5. Consistent challenges and usable playtest evidence

New daily and weekly offers have `:guard1` record IDs and use current route rewards, Owl support and tower rules. Old offer IDs recreate old rules exactly. Challenges retain separate results and the original no-retry scoring.

The local playtest dashboard distinguishes current Guard attempts from legacy records. It reports first-build timing, explicit resumes, route-plan use, retry attempts, losses around waves 8–10, paused time and different tower combinations. Counts remain device-local and do not establish player understanding or a retention rate.

## Verification

`npm run check` passes: TypeScript, 75 original simulation checks, 11 release checks, growing-canal and Guard suites, 11 journey groups and 15 exact original replay comparisons. Journey coverage includes interrupted migration, per-slot credit, exact checkpoint resources, Harbour transition and mid-wave restore, all eight Harbour waves, one-time Warden surge, Ember save continuity and its damage trade-off.

The reproducible matrix is in [JOURNEY-BALANCE.json](JOURNEY-BALANCE.json), generated by `npm run balance:journey` with seed 7:

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Standard, three strategies × two guardians | 6/6 complete the canal and Harbour |
| Nightfall, three strategies × two guardians | 4/6 complete both; balanced builds lose during the canal |
| Successful continuations that keep investing | 10/10 win; all use the four upstream pads |
| Same successful defences frozen at dawn | 4/10 win (beam builds); six lose at the Harbour Warden |
| Current daily and weekly with beam strategy | Both complete |

These are deterministic strategy probes, not human win rates. The existing strong beam strategy remains viable without further spending. Harbour does not force every successful player to rebuild.

Muted browser checks cover guided placement, independent saves, failure/retry/reload, preserved Harbour towers and new-pad construction, view switching, 375×812 and 320×700 portrait with larger text, and the scrolling dock at 812×375 landscape. QA fixtures are development-only and back up the previous test data.

## Remaining release gates

- Observe new players explaining placement and enemy routes without coaching, then returning voluntarily to the same defence. Keep the existing target of 8/10 unaided first placements as a target, not a result.
- Build, sign and profile the app on a Mac and physical iPhones. Windows can build and sync the web assets but cannot validate touch feel, native frame pacing, heat, haptics, interruption handling or TestFlight distribution. Use [IOS-BUILD.md](IOS-BUILD.md).
- Review the optional guardian with players before adding more characters, currencies or power upgrades.
